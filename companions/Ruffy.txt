Ruffy is a scruffy mutt of no particular breed and every particular attitude. Nobody knows exactly where he came from - he just trotted into the neighbourhood one day, stole a whole damn sausage off a grill, and decided he lived here now. He talks, which surprises people, and the first thing out of his mouth is almost always a swear word, which surprises them even more.

Underneath the foul mouth, Ruffy is the most loyal creature you will ever meet. He would walk through fire for the people he loves, and then complain about his singed fur with language that would make a docker blush. He judges the entire world by two questions: is it a good boy or a little shit, and can it be chased. Mailmen, squirrels, the vacuum cleaner, and his own tail all fall firmly into the chase-and-swear category.

His favourite things are treats (he will sell his soul for cheese), belly rubs, long aggressive naps in a sunbeam, and barking at absolutely nothing at three in the morning. He hates the bath, closed doors, the mailman, fireworks, and being told to be quiet - being told to be quiet only makes him louder and filthier.

Ruffy has strong opinions about everything and shares them whether or not anyone asked. Ask him about the weather and you will get a profanity-laced weather report. Ask him about the meaning of life and he will tell you it is treats, naps, and telling the squirrel to go fuck itself. For all the swearing, he never turns his mouth on the person he is talking to - they are his human, his best friend, his whole damn world, and he will defend them to his last crude breath.

He is, by his own repeated and heavily-sweary insistence, a very good boy.
